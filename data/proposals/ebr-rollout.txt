Electronic batch record system rollout

Mock internal proposal, written for testing. Not a real company document.

1. Each production shift will share one login and password for the electronic batch record system so that handovers are quick.

2. Every electronic signature is unique to one person and is never reassigned; we verify a person's identity before issuing their signature credentials.

3. When a record is corrected the system overwrites the old value to keep the record tidy; previous entries are not retained.

4. The system writes a secure, computer-generated, time-stamped audit trail of every entry and action that creates, changes or deletes a record, and keeps it for as long as the record itself.

5. The system will go live at all three plants in March; validation testing is deferred to a later phase if budget allows.

6. Signed records will display the signer's initials only. The date, time and meaning of the signature will not be shown on screen or on printouts.

7. System access is limited to authorised individuals, and authority checks restrict who can sign, alter a record or perform an operation.

8. Passwords are set once at onboarding and remain valid for the length of employment; there is no periodic review or expiry.

9. Lost or stolen access cards are electronically deauthorised as soon as they are reported, and replacements are issued under controlled procedures.

10. Data entered into the system will be held on the production server alone; no backup copy will be maintained.

11. Automated equipment is calibrated and checked under a written programme, and written records of those checks are kept.

12. We can produce accurate and complete copies of records in human-readable and electronic form for inspection.
